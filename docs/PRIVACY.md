# Privacy Notice

CarbonLens stores only the project name, submitted project polygon, verification scores, and testnet transaction hashes needed to demonstrate the service. It does not create user accounts or intentionally collect personal data.

Project data is retained for the duration needed to operate and evaluate this demo. Maintainers may delete or aggregate local records when the demo is retired, subject to any competition requirements.

Evidence published to IPFS is public and immutable: do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information in a project name, polygon, or supporting data.

The service may use third-party providers for satellite data, blockchain RPC access, IPFS pinning, bot protection, error monitoring, optional LLM explanations, and database hosting. Their privacy practices apply to data they process. We do not sell submitted data.

This notice describes a hackathon demonstration and may change as the project evolves.
